## Available Models

Currently available models:
- **Fish Audio S2** (Recommended: `s2.1-pro`) - Latest generation with best performance

## Deprecated Models
- **s1** - S1 is deprecated and will be retired on December 31, 2026. After that date, requests that specify `s1` are served by `s2.1-pro`. See [Migrate from S1](/developer-guide/models-pricing/migrate-from-s1).
- **speech-1.6** - Fish Speech v1.6 has been deprecated on February, 28th, 2026
- **speech-1.5** - Fish Speech v1.5 has been deprecated on February, 28th, 2026

<Note>
  We recommend using **`s2.1-pro`** for all new projects. If you use `s1`,
  follow [Migrate from S1](/developer-guide/models-pricing/migrate-from-s1)
  before December 31, 2026.
</Note>
