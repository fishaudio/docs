Fish Audio Enterprise can be deployed into infrastructure you control: your own
cloud account, an on-premise data center, or a network with no internet access at
all. Fish Audio delivers each model's container images and Helm chart through a
private registry; you own the cluster, the network boundary, and the data.

The self-hosted stack runs the same speech engine as the hosted API, so requests,
audio formats, and voice behavior match what you already build against.

## Why self-host

| Reason                  | What it gives you                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Colocation              | Run inference in the same region, VPC, or rack as your application and remove public-internet round trips from time-to-first-audio.     |
| Single-tenant isolation | Dedicated GPUs and queues. Capacity is not shared with other tenants, and you decide when the deployment is upgraded.                   |
| Security posture        | Voice traffic never leaves your network. The offline modes need no outbound access at runtime and run on disconnected networks. |
| Data sovereignty        | Input text, generated audio, and reference voices stay inside your boundary and under your own retention policy.                        |

## Platforms

Each model ships on two platforms. Your agreement decides which ones your team gets,
and in which modes; **Developer → Self Host** shows them.

|            | Helm (Kubernetes)                                                 | All-in-One (Docker)                                                              |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| What it is | The model's chart, installed on your cluster                      | One container on a single machine                                                |
| Best for   | Production clusters and elastic capacity                          | Evaluation, fixed-size appliances, strict air gaps                               |
| Scaling    | Replicas across GPUs and nodes, with optional autoscaling         | Add containers behind your own load balancer; each is one worker and one vocoder |
| Metrics    | Prometheus metrics from the speech services                       | API metrics only, at `/metrics`                                                  |
| Timestamps | Optional, where available                                         | Not included                                                                     |
| Guide      | [Kubernetes deployment](/developer-guide/self-hosting/kubernetes) | [All-in-One container](/developer-guide/self-hosting/all-in-one)                 |

The GPUs each model needs are on [Models](/developer-guide/self-hosting/models). On
either platform, ingress, DNS, TLS, external load balancing, and network allowlists
are yours to choose and configure.

## Billing

Both platforms come in either billing mode:

|                           | Online                                                                    | Offline                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Usage                     | Authorized and billed per request against Fish Audio                      | Recorded in a signed ledger on your side and reconciled with Fish Audio                                  |
| Network needed at runtime | Outbound HTTPS to Fish Audio                                              | None, so it can run air-gapped — see [Air-gapped deployments](/developer-guide/self-hosting/air-gapped) |
| Request token             | A [Fish Audio API key](/developer-guide/getting-started/api-key) with credit | Any stable, non-empty token, one per tenant, recorded as the billing identity and not checked: control access in front of the deployment |

## Supported APIs

A self-hosted deployment serves:

- **Text to speech** over [HTTP](/api-reference/endpoint/openapi-v1/text-to-speech)
  and [WebSocket streaming](/api-reference/endpoint/websocket/tts-live).
- **Reference voices.** A request that names a voice by `reference_id` uses that
  voice's archive on the deployment. Your team's fish.audio voices and Fish Audio's
  official voices get there by hand, or over HTTPS through the optional voice sync;
  the hosted voice library is never read at request time.
- **[Word and segment timestamps](/api-reference/endpoint/openapi-v1/text-to-speech-stream-with-timestamps)**,
  optional. They need one more GPU; contact Fish Audio to enable them.

Other Fish Audio products are hosted-only unless your agreement says otherwise.

## Getting access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Agree the deployment scope">
    Self-hosting is enabled per team under an enterprise agreement. Reach out
    through [fish.audio/enterprise](https://fish.audio/enterprise) with your GPU
    target, expected concurrency, and whether you need air-gapped operation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your team enabled">
    Once your agreement is in place, Fish Audio grants your team the models and
    modes it is entitled to, and the charts and images that go with them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a deploy token">
    Sign in to fish.audio and open **Developer → Self Host** to see the
    models, platforms, and modes your team is granted and to create the token that
    authenticates against the registry. See
    [Registry & license](/developer-guide/self-hosting/registry-access).
  </Step>
</Steps>
